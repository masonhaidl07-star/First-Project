The 6 Pillars

Master these and everything else takes care of itself.

🧴

Skincare

  • Cleanse twice a day — morning and night
  • SPF every morning. The #1 anti-aging move
  • Moisturize after every cleanse
  • Hands off your face — don't pick
💈

Hair & Grooming

  • Find a good barber, ask what suits your face shape
  • Keep the neckline and edges tight between cuts
  • Brows: clean up strays only — keep it natural
  • Facial hair: line it up or shave it clean
🏋️

Fitness

  • Lift 3–4x a week — compound movements first
  • Protein with every meal
  • Walk 8k+ steps a day
  • The physique is built in the kitchen
🦷

Teeth & Breath

  • Brush twice, floss once — every day
  • Tongue scraper changes the game
  • Whitening strips if you want the extra step
  • Don't skip the dentist
😴

Sleep & Nutrition

  • 7–9 hours, same schedule every night
  • 2–3 liters of water a day
  • Whole foods 80% of the time
  • Cut the sugary drinks first
🧍

Style & Posture

  • Fit beats brand — clothes that actually fit you
  • Build a base of neutral colors first
  • Stand tall: shoulders back, chin level
  • Dead hangs and face pulls fix desk posture

The Rules

The mindset that makes everything above actually work.

  1. Consistency beats intensity. Give any change 30 days before judging it. The routine only works if you do.
  2. Health first, always. Anything that damages your body isn't maxxing — it's the opposite. Skip the extreme stuff.
  3. Sleep is the cheat code. Skin, gym recovery, mood, focus — everything works better on 8 hours.
  4. Confidence compounds. Posture, eye contact and a calm voice do more than any product you can buy.
  5. Compare yourself to yesterday-you. Not to filtered strangers on the internet. Run your own race.
